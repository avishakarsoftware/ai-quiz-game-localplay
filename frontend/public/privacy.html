1. Data We Collect

What we collect depends on how you use the Service. You can play as a guest without creating an account.

Always collected

  • Nickname and avatar — chosen by you when joining a game session.
  • Game content you enter — topics, prompts, questions, drawings, and answers you submit. Topics and prompts are sent to a third-party AI service to generate game content.
  • A device identifier — a random identifier generated by the app and stored on your device. It keeps your Spark balance attached to your device. It is not your hardware serial number or an advertising identifier.
  • Usage and analytics data — pages visited, game events, device type, browser information, interaction patterns, and performance metrics, associated with anonymous or pseudonymous identifiers.
  • Technical data — IP addresses, request headers, and connection metadata automatically transmitted when you access the Service.

Collected only if you sign in (optional — Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple)

  • Email address, as provided by that sign-in provider. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only ever receive Apple's relay address.
  • A provider account identifier — the identifier the provider uses for your account, so we can recognize you when you sign in again.

We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising identifiers. Signing in lets your Sparks and purchases follow you across devices.

Collected only if you make a purchase

  • Purchase records — which Spark pack was bought, the amount credited, a transaction reference from the payment provider, and a timestamp. We keep this ledger to deliver what you paid for, prevent duplicate credits, and handle refunds.

We never receive or store your card number, bank details, or billing address. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe (web) or by Apple and Google (in-app purchases on mobile).

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data described above to:

  • Operate and deliver the Service, including running game sessions
  • Generate game content from the topics and prompts you provide
  • Maintain your Spark balance and deliver purchases you have made
  • Recognize you across devices when you choose to sign in
  • Analyze usage to maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Service
  • Detect, prevent, and address abuse, fraud, or security issues
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. We may publish or share aggregated or anonymized statistics that do not identify you.

6. Purchases and Sparks

The Service is free to play. Some features — hosting a game and generating AI content — consume "Sparks", a virtual in-app item. You receive free Sparks on a recurring basis and may optionally buy more.

  • Spark purchases are one-time purchases, not subscriptions. Nothing recurs automatically.
  • Sparks have no monetary value, cannot be exchanged for cash, and cannot be transferred between accounts.
  • Purchases made in the mobile apps are processed by Apple or Google and are subject to their refund policies. Web purchases are processed by Stripe.
  • If a purchase is refunded or charged back, the corresponding Sparks may be removed from your balance.
  • Prices are shown before you confirm any purchase, in your store's local currency where applicable.
